## Concrete speaker-chat gap

In [Ollama.answer](../backend/pipeline/structure.py), the prompt serializes each excerpt as `[segment_id] text`. In the [chat endpoint](../backend/main.py), `answer_question` receives `job.segments` but not `job.speakers`. The retrieval scorer also operates on words rather than speaker identity.

A no-inference probe used two otherwise identical transcripts with the two speaker IDs swapped. The complete chat prompt was **identical** before and after the swap. This demonstrates missing input information, not a model benchmark. Questions based on names explicitly spoken in the text can still work; questions based only on diarized or renamed voices are not fully supported.

Completion requires passing stable speaker IDs and displayed names into chat context, retaining relevant speaker turns during retrieval, and checking the answer's citations against the requested speaker. Verify anonymous and renamed voices, short and long recordings, and unknown/overlapping speech.

## Remaining work, in priority order

1. **Resolve and evaluate the known accuracy gaps:** missed spoken deadlines and conditional work, unsupported open questions, and incorrect/missing evidence. Use unfamiliar recordings with human-reviewed expected outcomes; do not equate matched quotations with correct meaning.
2. **Complete speaker-aware chat**, including renamed voices and citations attributable to the requested speaker.
3. **Evaluate human Kazakh and mixed RU/KZ/EN audio**, including names, dates, corrections, overlap, explicit risks, and a no-task/no-risk negative case. Report per-field errors rather than only successful job completion.
4. **Run the full disconnected rehearsal on the actual presentation laptop**, with its microphone, intended speaker backend, cold/repeated jobs, memory measurements, exports, and restart persistence. Mac results cannot certify the RTX machine or a noisy venue.
5. **Finish and rehearse the team pitch/live demonstration.** The repository contains the organizers' deck and [pitch plan](PITCH_DEMO.md), not a verified final team presentation. The plan still needs measured rehearsals with unfamiliar audio; it cannot promise a finished report at the exact end of a five-minute recording.

Additional unfinished options are pasted/text-transcript input, PDF export, automatic real-name inference, dense embeddings, and long-meeting chunking. Text transcripts appear as an alternative in the task description but are not a separate mandatory item on slide 3. None of the other options is necessary to satisfy the listed alternatives. Current 30-minute/350 MB limits do not guarantee a dense transcript fits the model context.

## Scoring interpretation

The official allocation is functionality **30**, AI accuracy **20**, locality **20**, bonuses **15**, UI/performance **10**, and presentation **5**. Individual bonuses have no published separate point allocation. An exact expected score or completion percentage would therefore be speculative. The largest remaining concerns are evidence of accurate outcomes and a complete offline demonstration on the target machine, followed by the specific speaker-chat gap.
